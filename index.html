Shopelligence is a business-specific AI chatbot you can train to represent your brand. It handles the entire customer journey from discovery to payment confirmation.

Sales-Focused by Design
Every conversation is steered toward a completed order — not just a response.
Intelligent Interaction
Searches inventory by image or text, answers questions, and recommends based on customer preferences.
End-to-End Order Flow
